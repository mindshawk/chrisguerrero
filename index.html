About Chris

Selma, CA to Austin, TX.

One of BuzzFeed's early video directors: helped create Tasty and The Try Guys, made comedy at Super Deluxe, directed Funny Or Die commercials, and launched Dwayne Johnson's YouTube channel as director and EP.

2025 Frieze Los Angeles Film Award fellow (his short Reprogrammed won the Audience Award). Adjunct directing professor at USC.

Chicano writer-director from Selma, California, a Central Valley raisin town.

Funny until it isn't.

A video store. A kitchen timer. A jacket that never comes back.

A broke Echo Park video-store lifer and his music-exec girlfriend, who shelved her bass to pay rent, break up, but their building was sold, so they try 30 days apart under one roof. Then he learns the director they both depend on is abusing a young woman and stealing her work, and saving his store and her Burbank house means staying quiet.
